# Global Supply Chain Updates - Q3
The Nexus Group has recently faced severe delays in their microchip division. 
This delay was caused by a fire at the Munich Foundry, which is owned by Nexus Group.
Dr. Aris Thorne, the lead engineer at the Munich Foundry, reported that the fire damaged the primary lithography machines.
As a result of this damage, Nexus Group's main client, OmniCorp, has paused all new orders for Q4.
OmniCorp is now looking into alternative suppliers, specifically Horizon Tech.
