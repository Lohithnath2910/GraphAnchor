The heavy metallurgical industrial sector of the Ruhr Valley in western Germany has a rich history spanning over two centuries of iron smelting, precision casting, and composite alloy fabrication. While traditional steel mills dominated the 20th century, the region has transitioned toward advanced materials engineering and vacuum-arc metallurgical processing. One of the most technologically advanced facilities operating in this corridor is the Prometheus Advanced Foundry, situated in an industrial complex just south of Düsseldorf.

The Prometheus Advanced Foundry is managed by Executive Director Klara Lindqvist, who previously served as head of precision metallurgy at Aker Solutions in Gothenburg. The foundry specializes in extreme-temperature vacuum sintering and holds the exclusive fabrication subcontract for Aerodyne's Xenon-Helium Plasma Cores. These delicate electromagnetic cores are forged inside super-pressurized Titanium-Cobalt containment chambers to prevent atmospheric oxidation. In addition to its aerospace contracts, Prometheus Advanced Foundry operates a separate commercial manufacturing line that produces modular extruded aluminum heat sinks for industrial refrigeration units distributed across Western Europe.
