The Hudson Valley in Upstate New York possesses rich alluvial soil and a temperate microclimate ideal for temperate pomology and deciduous fruit cultivation. Operating since 1912 across two hundred rolling acres near Rhinebeck, the Hudson Valley Apple Orchard is a heritage agricultural estate owned and actively managed by Farmer Robert Miller. The orchard specializes in cultivating heritage crisp apple cultivars, focusing primarily on Honeycrisp, Gala, and Northern Spy apple varieties.

Farmer Robert Miller adheres to regenerative agricultural principles, fertilizing the root zones of the apple trees exclusively with aged organic mushroom compost and nitrogen-fixing clover cover crops rather than synthetic chemicals. During the peak harvest season in late September, the orchard employs thirty seasonal workers to hand-pick the ripe apples, which are graded for quality and washed in spring water. The premium first-grade apples are packaged for fresh wholesale markets throughout New York City, while the second-grade apples are pressed on-site in a traditional wooden screw press to produce unfiltered artisanal apple cider, which is fermented in oak barrels and sold at local farmers' markets.
