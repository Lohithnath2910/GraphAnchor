Spanish: The Verb Estar (To Be)

ESTAR = To be Like SER verb, when you use a form of ESTAR, then you are referring to: IS, AM, and ARE. However, there is still a difference between SER and ESTAR based on their uses. Yo estoy Nosotros/as estamos Tú estás Vosotros estáis Él está Ellos están Ella está Ellas están Usted está Ustedes están María está María y Pablo están
Location E.g. Nosotros estamos en la clase. Physical Condition E.g. Mi madre está cansada. Emotion E.g. Los estudiantes están felices. ESTAR verb is always used when we want to express anyone's emotion. To express states of feeling, we should use different adjectives with ESTAR verb. While using adjectives for expressing feelings and emotions, we must remember to change the adjectives according to the number and gender of the nouns/ pronouns with which it is being used.
