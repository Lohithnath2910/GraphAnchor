Spanish: Greetings and Saying Goodbye (Saludar y Despedirse)

Good Morning! Good afternoon/ Good evening! Good Night! How are you? How's it going? What's up? I am well. I am bad. I am so-so. And you? What is your name? (Informal) My name is Pablo.

Where are you from? I am from …. Nice to meet you. The pleasure is mine. Delighted to meet you. Likewise. See you later. See you tomorrow. Good-bye. In general, speak formally with people: you don't know older than you In a superior position (like your boss, teacher or principal) What is your name? How are you? Where are you from?
